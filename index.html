From the journal

Take the scenic route.

A good trip is more than a list of stops. It is the early light on a trail, a warm meal after a long walk, and enough time to take the path that caught your eye.

Elsewhere is a sample journal for curious travelers. We collect practical notes and personal stories that make it easier to plan a day outdoors, leave a little room for detours, and notice the details that make a place feel like its own.

Our field notes are made for real days outside: check the forecast, carry water, choose a route that suits your pace, and let someone know where you are going. Then take your time. Stop for the view. Turn back when it feels right. The point is not to cover the most ground; it is to come home with a story you want to tell.

Pack for the day

A simple checklist for changing weather, comfortable layers, water, snacks, and the small essentials that help a day go smoothly.

Choose your own pace

Pick a route that leaves room for pauses. A good outing should fit the people, energy, and time you have today.

Leave no trace

A few thoughtful habits help keep wild places welcoming: stay on durable paths, pack out what you bring, and give wildlife space.
